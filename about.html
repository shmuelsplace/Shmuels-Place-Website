Our Story

Why I Founded Shmuel's Place Global

I built this firm around something I saw up close, working inside the operations of busy executives: the people carrying the most responsibility are often the ones with the least time to manage the two things that shape how they're perceived and how they perform — their schedule and their presence.

I had spent time on both sides of that gap — supporting executives as an operations professional, and helping people find clarity and confidence in how they present themselves through styling. It became clear these were never really two separate skills. They were one discipline, split across two vendors, forcing busy principals to manage the split themselves.

Shmuel's Place Global exists to close that gap — to be the one relationship a founder, executive, or principal can trust with both their time and their image, held to the same standard of discretion and care.

— Adaramaja Ayotunde Samuel, Founder
