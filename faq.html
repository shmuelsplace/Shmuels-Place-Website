Frequently Asked Questions

Answers before you ask.

If your question isn't here, it's a good reason to start a conversation directly.

What exactly is "Executive Operations Support"?+

It is the strategic management of your time, travel, communications, and operating environment — going beyond a traditional PA role by anticipating needs and holding decision-making authority, not just executing tasks handed to you.

How is this different from hiring a personal assistant or a stylist separately?+

A PA or stylist each solve half of the problem, and you still have to manage two vendors. Shmuel's Place Global holds both disciplines in one relationship, with one point of contact and one standard of judgment applied to your calendar and your closet together.

Do I need to interview for this?+

No. Every engagement begins with a private discovery conversation led by us, not a pitch you evaluate. We ask the questions, and we tell you plainly whether the fit is right for both sides.

How much does this cost?+

Pricing depends entirely on scope and tier, and is discussed only after your discovery conversation — it is never published as a fixed rate card, since every engagement is tailored.

Is my information confidential?+

Yes. Discretion is a core pillar of this firm. Confidentiality agreements are standard on every engagement, and client details are never shared publicly without written permission.

Where are you currently operating?+

We are currently based in Ogun State, Nigeria, with an active expansion plan into Lagos and Abuja. Engagements involving travel or remote coordination beyond these locations can be discussed during your discovery conversation.

How do I get started?+

Submit a request through our contact form, or reach us directly by phone, WhatsApp, or email. We'll follow up to schedule your discovery conversation.

Still have a question?

Reach out directly — we respond personally to every enquiry.
