Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Shmuel's Place Global Ltd ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and your rights regarding it. This is a general-purpose policy template and should be reviewed by a licensed lawyer in your operating jurisdiction before formal publication.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a request through our contact forms, we collect the information you provide directly, which may include your full name, company or title, email address, phone number, the service you are enquiring about, and any message you include.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your enquiry and schedule a discovery conversation.
  • To assess fit for our services and, where an engagement proceeds, to deliver them.
  • To maintain records necessary for confidentiality and service quality.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Third-Party Form Processing

Our contact and request forms are processed using FormSubmit, a third-party form-delivery service that forwards your submission directly to our email address. We recommend reviewing FormSubmit's own privacy practices for details on how submissions are transmitted.

4. Confidentiality

Discretion is a core value of this firm. Information shared with us — whether through this website or during an engagement — is treated as confidential and is not disclosed publicly without written permission, except where required by law.

5. Cookies

This website does not currently use tracking cookies or third-party analytics beyond standard hosting-provider logs.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have submitted to us by contacting us at officeofshmuel@gmail.com.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above will reflect the most recent revision.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to officeofshmuel@gmail.com or +234 814 751 0070.
